Each row is one physical tuner on the SAT>IP device. The src= number maps directly to a DiSEqC port on the LNB switch — the SAT>IP device handles the switch commands automatically. DiSEqC port and Satellite are informational labels to help you identify your wiring; leave blank for a single-LNB setup.
